About

Code, data, and open source

I am a software developer and speaker best known for my work on the YAML data serialization language.

Recently I created YAMLScript, a programming language that embeds cleanly into YAML and adds the functional features many YAML users need.

I maintain many important YAML open source projects and enjoy turning ambitious language ideas into practical tools.

Work with me

I am available for consulting, training, and speaking engagements worldwide on YAML, YAMLScript, and related topics.
